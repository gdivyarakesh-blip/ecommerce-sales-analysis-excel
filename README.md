# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce sales dataset using **Microsoft Excel** to identify sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer behavior, and order status.

An interactive dashboard was created to convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Compare sales performance across product categories
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze order status distribution
- Compare customer sales by gender
- Create an interactive and decision-ready Excel dashboard

---

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Electronics** generated the highest Final Sales at approximately **₹8.49M**.
- **November** recorded the highest monthly Final Sales at approximately **₹1.75M**.
- **Smartphone** was the highest-selling product at approximately **₹3.91M** in Final Sales.
- **Delivered orders** accounted for **87.2%** of total orders.
- The business generated approximately **₹3.51M profit** with an overall **22.56% profit margin**.
- Male customers contributed approximately **₹8.29M** in Final Sales, while female customers contributed approximately **₹7.26M**.
